The camera keeps the person.

You already point a camera at the cashier and the counter. That shows who was there. It does not show which button they pressed, what total they rang, or where a mistake started.

This keeps the screen.

Install the app on the point-of-sale tablet. When the screen changes, a clip starts. When the screen sits still, it stops. Open the site to see the live picture or replay the moment.

How a shift gets recorded

  1. 01

    Put the app on the tablet

    The register phone or tablet is only for the point of sale. Install Till Recorder on that device.

  2. 02

    Arm it once

    Choose Entire screen when Android asks. After that, it watches on its own. A notice stays visible so the person at the register can see it is on.

  3. 03

    Watch from here

    Live view while the screen is in use. Turn the tablet’s front camera on from this site when you want the person and the screen in one view, and turn it off when you are done. Playback shows exactly what was on the display.

Install the tablet app

Download the APK, open it on the Android register, and arm that device. Clips are stored in Cloudflare. This website is where you watch them.
